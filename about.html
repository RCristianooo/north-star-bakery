Our Story

North Star Bakery is proudly known for freshly prepared bread, pastries, and cakes. We started in our little kitchen with a huge passion for baking. Friends and family loved it enough that we started a business dedicated to serving both our current and new customers.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

North Star Bakery ensures in choosing quality ingredients to create fresh and fragrant food for our customers to enjoy. We always strive for freshness, flavor, and locally sourced ingredients to achieve the best results.

The Team

Every piece of bread, pastry, or cake is a person behind the creation. We, North Star Bakery, have a team that has a genuine heart for baking. We hope our customers can enjoy the good food from North Star Bakery.
